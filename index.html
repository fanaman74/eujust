05 Method / limits

Read the fine print.

An informed reading starts here. The interface is a reading aid; the linked official document is always the authority.

Note 01

The official text is the authority

Case records link to EUR-Lex. Other items link to the institution that published them. Titles are shown as published.

source first
Note 02

Summaries are labelled

AI summaries are optional, marked as generated and kept apart from the official record. They are a reading aid, never a court finding.

never a finding
Note 03

The collection has limits

Rulings are found by keyword searches over a bounded window, and topics are keyword matches rather than legal analysis. Relevant material may be missing.

not exhaustive
