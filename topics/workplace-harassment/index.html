01 Read it in context

Context matters.

A harassment keyword can appear in a judgment without the court finding that harassment occurred. The court may be considering procedure, evidence, an employment decision or another legal question. The official judgment explains which questions it actually decided.

The monitor uses English keyword matches over a bounded discovery window. Relevant cases may be missing, and matched topics are not legal analysis.
