01 Read it in context

Context matters.

Equal-treatment questions depend on the facts and the law applied in each case. A topic match does not establish that discrimination occurred or that the same result applies to another situation. Use the court’s own reasons and decision when comparing records.

The monitor uses English keyword matches over a bounded discovery window. Relevant cases may be missing, and matched topics are not legal analysis.
