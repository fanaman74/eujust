04 Explainer

What discharge means.

Budgetary discharge is Parliament’s annual political scrutiny of how EU money was managed and implemented. Parliament reviews the accounts, audit findings, recommendations and the responsible institutions’ answers before deciding whether to grant, postpone or refuse discharge.

It is a budget accountability process. It is separate from staff or employment procedures, and a discharge decision does not by itself establish that every irregularity has been resolved.

Treaty provision Financial Regulation 2024/2509
  1. 01
    Evidence

    Accounts, audit findings and recommendations are assembled.

  2. 02
    Answers

    Institutions provide replies and supporting follow-up material.

  3. 03
    Decision

    Parliament grants, postpones or refuses discharge.

  4. 04
    Follow-up

    Later reports track responses and outstanding requests.
