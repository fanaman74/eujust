# EU JUST design system

Use paper `#F8F4E8`, ink `#09090B`, and acid yellow-green `#D2E823`. Dela Gothic One gives display headings and the wordmark their weight. Space Grotesk carries body text and controls. IBM Plex Mono is used for case identifiers, dates, and source metadata. Fonts are self-hosted.

The shared sticky navigation sits in an inset paper frame. Buttons, cards, and archive controls use two-pixel ink borders, with hard offset shadows for elevation. Accent color marks actions and highlights. The footer uses an ink surface with paper text.

The homepage pairs a large headline with a credited photograph of the Court of Justice and an overlapping featured ruling. The research links form an asymmetrical grid. The latest-rulings rail uses real case numbers, dates, courts, topics, and official source links. On narrow screens the hero stacks, and the rail supports touch, keyboard, and arrow buttons.

Motion includes the highlights marquee, the floating featured record, a brief headline glitch, and a pointer-following accent on mouse input. Reduced-motion mode presents static content. Focus stays visible, the native pointer stays available, and the marquee can be paused.

The official document is the authority. Retain case data, keyword-match context, bounded-coverage language, access to search and monitoring, and attribution for published assets.
