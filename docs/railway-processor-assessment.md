# msoit — Railway processor assessment

Reviewed 29 October 2026 for EU JUST. Customer information supplied: msoit, Belgium, hello@msoit.eu. The owner confirmed Railway Hobby. The website application's successful Amsterdam deployment was verified. This document records reviewed public terms; it is not an executed agreement or a legal-compliance certificate.

## What can be confirmed

- Railway publishes a standard, self-service DPA for customers. Its published DPA execution instructions do not restrict that agreement to Enterprise. Hobby status therefore does not, on the reviewed evidence, require an upgrade merely to use the standard signing process. Some additional audit/security documents or services may have separate access or plan conditions.
- The DPA identifies Railway Corporation as processor of customer Personal Data (or subprocessor where its customer acts as processor). It separately treats Company Account Data and Company Usage Data as Railway's independent-controller processing. A review must account for both roles rather than assume every Railway log is processed solely on msoit's instructions.
- The published transfer provisions state that primary processing operations take place in the United States, while local storage options may be offered. Selecting Amsterdam does not remove the need to assess support, platform data and onward processing.
- For relevant EEA transfers, the standard terms provide DPF reliance only to the extent the recipient is certified, or incorporate the 2021 EU SCCs. Module Two is the stated controller-to-processor module; Module One and Three apply to the other specified roles. No current certification status was established in this review, and no executed SCCs for msoit have been produced.
- The terms include subprocessor notice/objection provisions, assistance with rights requests and breaches, technical measures, and return/deletion provisions. They refer to the Trust Center for the current subprocessor list.
- Railway's Hobby documentation provides seven days of log history. It explicitly says plan upgrades can restore older logs. This is not a verified seven-day erasure commitment.
